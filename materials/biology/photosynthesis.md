# Chapter 4: Photosynthesis

<!-- page: 2 -->

Photosynthesis converts light energy into chemical energy stored in glucose.
Chlorophyll in the chloroplast absorbs the light used by the light reactions.
The Calvin cycle builds sugar and does not itself require darkness; it needs products of the light reactions.
Carbon dioxide and water are the inputs, and glucose and oxygen are the outputs.
The leaf's stomata let carbon dioxide in and oxygen out.
