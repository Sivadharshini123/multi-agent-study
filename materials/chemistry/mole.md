# Chapter 1: The mole

<!-- page: 4 -->

One mole contains Avogadro's number of particles, 6.022 x 10^23.
Molar mass is the mass of one mole of a substance, in grams per mole.
Amount of substance in moles equals mass divided by molar mass.
n = m / M is the working relation between moles, mass, and molar mass.
A balanced chemical equation states the mole ratio of reactants and products.
